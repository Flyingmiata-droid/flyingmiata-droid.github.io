How the score works

Rules-based v1 starting at 50, capped 0–100. Higher score = stronger heavy-equipment-purchase signal.

  • +20 $20M+ project value · +15 $10M+ · +10 $5M+ · +5 $1M+
  • +10 50K+ sqft · +5 10K+ sqft
  • +10 4+ floors · +5 2–3 floors
  • +5 work class is New, Shell, or Addition & Remodel
