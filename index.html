How It Works

Three layers. One platform.

From search to close, RigBrain handles the entire equipment lifecycle.

Layer 1

AI Search & Matching

Describe what you need in plain language. Our AI matches you with available inventory across dealers, rental fleets, auctions, and private sellers — ranked by fit, price, proximity, and total cost of ownership.

Layer 2

See real-time fair market values, depreciation curves, and comparable sales. Our AI negotiation agent handles back-and-forth with dealers on your behalf — optimizing price, warranty, delivery, and financing terms.
