What is RigBrain?

RigBrain is a permit-based lead generation platform for construction equipment dealers. Its core feature, PermitMatch, ingests public building permits from city open-data portals, classifies each permit by project type (commercial new-build, demolition, multi-family, tenant finish-out, etc.), and maps it to the equipment categories that type of job typically requires. We match the result to nearby dealers and deliver scored, contractor-ready leads every week. Each lead includes contractor name, phone number, project address, estimated valuation, and recommended equipment categories. We currently track over 20,000 real Austin building permits and are onboarding our first pilot dealers now.

How It Works

Permits in. Leads out.

PermitMatch does the work your team doesn't have time for.

Step 1

We Monitor Permits

RigBrain ingests building permits from public government APIs in real time — capturing address, GPS coordinates, project scope, valuation, and contractor info.

Step 2

Equipment Needs Tagged by Project Type

We classify each permit by project type — commercial new build, tilt-wall, demolition, tenant finish-out, multi-family — and map it to the equipment categories that job typically uses. A new school build gets tagged for excavators, cranes, and boom lifts. A tear-down gets skid steers and concrete saws. The classification gets sharper as dealer feedback flows in.

Step 3

Leads Matched to You

Permits are matched to your dealership based on location, equipment categories, and project size. Each lead is scored 0-100 so you know where to focus.

Step 4

Weekly Report Delivered

Every week you get a lead report with contractor name, phone number, project details, and recommended equipment. Early in the project lifecycle is when equipment decisions get made — this gets you into that conversation.
