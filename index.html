What is RigBrain?

RigBrain is a permit-based lead generation platform for construction equipment dealers. Its core feature, PermitMatch, ingests public commercial building permits from city open-data portals (Austin and Orlando today, with eight more top-10 US metros activating as Founding Dealers sign), classifies each permit by project type (commercial new-build, demolition, multi-family, tenant finish-out, etc.), and maps it to the equipment categories that type of job typically requires. We match the result to nearby dealers and deliver scored, contractor-ready leads every week. Each lead includes contractor name, phone number, project address, estimated valuation, and recommended equipment categories. Austin and Orlando are live today; the remaining eight metros activate as Founding Dealers onboard.
